Numbo

Numerology meets your everyday life.

Coming soon to phones and tablets

Most numerology apps hand you fixed predictions. Numbo does something different: it sits alongside your habits, your diary and your spending, notes the numbers at the moment each thing happened, and then shows you the patterns in your own records — not someone else's forecast.

What's inside

Your numbers

Driver, conductor, name numbers, Lo Shu grid, pinnacles and more — for you and the people you care about.

Compatibility

Compare profiles and see how names, dates and numbers fit together, with every score explained.

Habit tracker

Build good habits, ease off the others, and see how your days line up with your numbers.

Diary

Write about your day; Numbo quietly records the numbers around each entry.

Money

Track income and spending, and find out whether certain days treat your wallet differently.

Remedies

Log what you try and whether it worked, so you learn what actually helps you.

Magic numbers

Note the numbers that keep appearing to you, and look back on when and where they showed up.

Reports

Tap any bar to see the exact records behind it — patterns you can check, not claims you have to trust.

Private by design

Your records never leave your device.
  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync. Numbo doesn't know who you are.
  • Works completely offline.
  • Encrypted backups, protected by a passphrase only you know — saved wherever you choose.

Free, with a one-time Premium

Numbo is free to use. A single one-time purchase unlocks deeper analysis and removes the banner ads — no subscription, nothing recurring. Your own records are never locked: every record stays readable and editable, and backup and restore are free for everyone, always.

Support

Found a problem, have a question, or an idea for Numbo? Write any time:

support@ajinkkya.com

Please mention your device model and the Numbo version (Menu → About) if you're reporting a problem. Never send your backup file or its passphrase — support never needs either.

Moving to a new phone?

In Numbo, open Settings → Backup & Restore on the old phone, create a backup and save it somewhere you can reach from the new one. Install Numbo on the new phone and restore it with the same passphrase.

Reinstalled Numbo and Premium is gone?

Open Settings → Numbo Premium → Restore purchase, signed in to the same store account you bought it with. Premium is tied to that account, not to the phone.

Does Numbo send my data anywhere?

Your records, never. If you allow it, Numbo can send an anonymous crash report so faults get fixed — it contains nothing you entered, and you can switch it off in Settings → Privacy. Free users also see banner ads.
